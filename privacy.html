Privacy Policy

Application: There's a Reason · Effective date: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the There's a Reason application ("the App", "we", "us") accesses, uses, stores, and shares data when it interacts with Google and YouTube services on behalf of its operator. The App is an automated video production and publishing pipeline that creates short-form videos and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Who this policy is for

The App is operated by an individual creator for the sole purpose of publishing videos to a YouTube channel that the operator owns and controls. The only person who authenticates with the App is the operator. The App does not offer accounts to, or collect data from, members of the public.

Data we access

When the operator authorizes the App through Google's OAuth consent flow, the App requests a single scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — used only to upload video files to the operator's own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API v3.

Through this authorization the App receives an OAuth access token and refresh token that identify the operator's Google Account for the purpose of uploading. The App does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, YouTube watch history, subscriptions, comments, or any other Google user data.

How we use the data

The access and refresh tokens are used exclusively to authenticate upload requests to YouTube. Video files, titles, descriptions, and related metadata produced by the pipeline are transmitted to YouTube so that the video can be published to the operator's channel. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing the operator's own content.

How we store the data

OAuth credentials (client secret and refresh token) are stored locally on the operator's own machine in a protected directory that is excluded from version control. Credentials are never transmitted to any third party other than Google for the purpose of authentication. No Google user data is stored on external servers operated by us.

How we share the data

We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with any third party. Data is shared only with Google and YouTube as required to perform uploads. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Because the App accesses only the operator's own account, the operator may revoke the App's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens. The operator may delete the locally stored credentials at any time, which permanently removes the App's ability to act on their behalf.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing the App, the operator is also agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. For information on how Google handles data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
