There's a Reason

Short explainers about things you see every day but never questioned — infrastructure, terrain, cities, and natural phenomena.

What this is

There's a Reason is a YouTube channel that answers a simple question about ordinary things: why is it like that? Why aren't runways flat? Why do rivers bend? Each short video takes one visible-but- unexplained detail of the world and walks through the reason behind it, in under a minute.

How it's made

The channel is produced by an automated pipeline that researches a topic, writes and fact-checks a script, generates narration and visuals, renders the video, and — after human review — publishes it to the channel via the YouTube Data API. The pipeline uploads only to its own channel and accesses no other account data.
